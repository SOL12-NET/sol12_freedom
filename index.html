SOL12 // Swiss Tor Relays for Privacy & Free Expression

Private by design.
Free by principle.

Across the European Union, proposals such as client-side scanning of private messages ("Chat Control") and calls for "lawful access" to encrypted data keep putting strong encryption under pressure. SOL12 exists to push back the most concrete way we know: by running infrastructure.

We fund, secure and operate Tor relays in Switzerland, outside the EU and under one of the most stable legal frameworks for privacy. Every relay adds capacity and diversity to the Tor network, the network that journalists, activists and ordinary people rely on to communicate without being watched.

// CORE PRINCIPLES

The SOL12 Manifesto

01

Unconditional Encryption

Strong encryption is not a loophole, it is a safeguard for fundamental rights. We oppose backdoors, deliberate weakening of encryption, and the indiscriminate scanning of personal devices and messages.

02

No Persistent Logs

Data that is never written down cannot be seized, leaked or abused. Our relays run in volatile memory (RAM) and keep no persistent logs.

03

Swiss Neutrality

Our servers are in Switzerland, outside the European Union, under a stable legal framework that protects privacy. Independent infrastructure is the alternative to mass surveillance.

04

Built for the Common Good

SOL12 is independent and self-funded. We give our time and resources to strengthen the global Tor network, with no commercial agenda and nothing to sell.

Privacy is not something to hide behind. It is the condition for speaking freely.
