// CORE PRINCIPLES

The SOL12 Manifesto

Four commitments governing our infrastructure and civic action.

01

Unconditional Encryption

Unbreakable encryption is a fundamental human right. We categorically reject any backdoors, algorithmic weakening, or preemptive scanning of personal devices.

02

Zero Disk Persistence

What is never recorded can never be compromised, seized, or leaked. Our nodes run entirely in volatile memory (RAM) and generate no persistent log files.

03

Sovereign Neutrality

Located in Switzerland, our servers benefit from a stable and protective legal framework outside the European Union, insulated from extraterritorial pressures and mass surveillance directives.

04

Dedicated to the Common Good

We operate these relays on an independent, self-funded volunteer basis to strengthen the resilience of the global Tor network for journalists, activists, and citizens worldwide.
