// CORE PRINCIPLES

The SOL12 Manifesto

Four principles guide our work and the infrastructure we operate.

01

Unconditional Encryption

Strong encryption protects fundamental rights. We reject backdoors, deliberate weakening of encryption, and indiscriminate scanning of personal devices.

02

No Persistent Logs

What is never recorded can never be compromised, seized, or leaked. Our nodes run entirely in volatile memory (RAM) and generate no persistent log files.

03

Sovereign Neutrality

Our servers are based in Switzerland, under a stable legal framework that protects privacy. We support independent infrastructure and oppose mass surveillance.

04

Dedicated to the Common Good

We run these relays independently, funding them ourselves and volunteering our time to strengthen the global Tor network for journalists, activists, and people worldwide.
