All fonts used in SOL12 Freedom (`freedom.sol12.net`) are strictly open-source, self-hosted, and licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1)**.
No third-party CDN or external servers are contacted. All font assets are bundled locally as static `woff2` files in compliance with strict Content-Security-Policy directives (`font-src 'self'`).
